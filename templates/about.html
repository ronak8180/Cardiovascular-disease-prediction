About Heart Disease Prediction

Understanding heart disease, risk factors, and how our AI system works

What is Heart Disease?

Definition

Heart disease refers to conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels, including coronary artery disease, heart attacks, and heart failure.

Prevalence

Heart disease is the leading cause of death worldwide, affecting millions of people each year. Early detection and prevention are crucial.

Prevention

Many heart diseases can be prevented through lifestyle changes, regular exercise, healthy eating, and avoiding smoking.

Risk Factors

Non-Modifiable Risk Factors

Age

Risk increases with age, especially after 65

Gender

Men are at higher risk, but women's risk increases after menopause

Family History

Genetic predisposition to heart disease

Modifiable Risk Factors

Smoking

Major risk factor that can be eliminated

Obesity

Excess weight increases heart disease risk

Physical Inactivity

Lack of exercise contributes to heart disease

Poor Diet

High sodium, saturated fats, and processed foods

High Blood Pressure

Can be managed with lifestyle changes and medication

High Cholesterol

Can be controlled through diet and medication

Frequently Asked Questions

No prediction system is 100% accurate. Our AI model has a 95% accuracy rate, but it should be used as a screening tool, not a definitive diagnosis. Always consult with healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

How often should I use this system?

We recommend using the system monthly or whenever you make significant lifestyle changes. Regular monitoring helps track your health progress and identify trends.
